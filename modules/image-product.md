# Image Annex · Faithful Product / Object (360 · Turnaround · Multi-View)
Loaded together with `image.md` (Parts A, B and E: arsenal, prompt, Pass³ and consistent execution; Part C when the scene is advertising).

## Faithful Product / Object by AI (Subject Reference + Product Turnaround Sheet 360 / Multi-View)

Used for aircraft, vehicle, packaging, machine, property or piece that requires exact fidelity to the client's real specimen.
**Exact technical name and quick triggers:** `Product Turnaround Sheet 360` (or `Multi-View Consistency Sheet`) for the board; `Product Spin 360°` (turntable) for the spin; `Exploded View` and `Cross-Section` for exploded view and cutaway (§4). Activates immediately with: `/360-product`, `/product-concept` (§5), `/designer-mestre /input` with a product (§5), `/designer-mestre 360`, `/designer-mestre turnaround`, `/designer-mestre multiview`, `/designer-mestre product`, `360 manual`, `360 model`, `360 spin`, `spin`, `turntable`.

### 1. Organization and Golden Rules
- **Files:** real photos in `[Cliente]\Compartilhada\Imagens\Originais\Produtos\[linha]\[item]\` (no line: `Originais\Produtos\[Produto]\`; read-only; Law 5); 360 manual dossier in `[Cliente]\_DESIGN\Produtos\[Produto]\PRODUTO_[NOME].md`; board and treated references in `[Cliente]\_DESIGN\Produtos\[Produto]\Referências\turnaround_[produto].jpg`.
- **Research at extraction and scale:** when extracting each product, research its information and specifications and save them in the same 360 package: identification, SKU/variant, physical dimensions with unit, weight, capacity, material and finish, with source and degree of confirmation. Unconfirmed fields stay pending. Before composing several products, consult these sheets and record the relative scale applied, without inferring measurements from pixels or file names.
- **References and priority:** follow U12. Before each round, list the product's local folders and classify the files by view, side and detail. **Order of packaging sources:** (1) the client's own original packaging file (high-quality PDF/AI art), with maximum weight and priority for extracting and applying label text, colors and layout; (2) without it, local product references (renders, photos and views in the client's archive); (3) only without either, internet research (official store, professional store, resellers), asking the CDN for the original file without a size parameter, because the storefront usually serves only the thumbnail. A lower-tier source only fills gaps and never contradicts a higher-tier one. Originals and safe sources prevail over synthetic images; an approved generated view only complements gaps. A material divergence between safe references requires clarifying the version/configuration before reconstructing; the absence of an angle is not a divergence.
- **Packaging quality is a priority:** label and artwork at the resolution and color of the best official source (PDF art > manufacturer render > photo); never deliver a label more faded or blurred than the source.
- **Technical research (understanding the product):** when the same view or part is wrong in two consecutive generations, besides asking for a real reference (`image.md` A §2), research the exact product name's datasheet and a general description of the format (category, dimensions, proportions, component layout) to understand what the product is. This guides the reading of local references and fills in only what they do not show; nothing aesthetic comes from the research.
- **Real photo first:** if the briefing requires a "real photo", use AI only with the user's explicit authorization recorded in `design_system.md`.
- **No real photo of the product:** with recorded authorization, the best generated and approved view becomes the **provisional `_gerada` master** (recorded in `PRODUTO_*.md`) until a real photo arrives; it never replaces an existing real photo.
- **Neutral background mandatory in the reference:** turnaround, `_ref` views and package details stay on a neutral colorless background (plain light gray `#EEEEEE–#F5F5F5` or white on a cutout), soft even light, no setting, colored gel, sky or brand gradient. Scenes (hangar, runway, road, room) are **outputs** and go to `Imagens\IA\`, never to the product's `Referências\`. In view generation: `isolated on a seamless neutral light-gray studio background, soft even light, soft contact shadow`.
- **Reference = the CLIENT'S specimen:** an external 3D model or photo of another specimen serves only as geometry support, never as master (paint, stripes, finishes and details come exclusively from the client's real photos). Never include registration, license plate, serial number, watermark or external logo.

### 1.1 Master curation (before assembling the package)
The package is only worth it if the references follow the standard of the client's own good photos. Score each candidate in a table in `PRODUTO_*.md` (file · px · role · score) and discard with a reason:
- **Accept:** **current** version/paint/packaging of the product; longer side ≥ 1500 px as master (1000 to 1500 px only as support; below 1000 px consultation only); whole, sharp product; even daylight or studio light; canonical angle (¾, profile, frontal); same configuration as the client's specimen.
- **Discard as master:** backlight/silhouette/sunset/night, blurred motion, tilted or partial crop, brand-manual composition/mockup/render, photo with watermark or third-party credit, another specimen/template, old version (becomes a marked historical reference).
- **Same standard across the whole package:** do not mix paints/versions on one sheet; an unconfirmed new version stays in a separate `_A_CONFIRMAR` file.
- No ideal candidate passed: use the best available references as a provisional base and proceed with U12 generation and approval. Ask for a new photo only if there is not enough identity, there is a material version conflict or the briefing requires a real record; do not block merely because the photo of that angle is missing.

### 2. Building the Standard, Multi-View Geometry and Turnaround Sheet (once per product)
1. **Selection of 4–8 real views and Multi-View Camera Parameters:** frontal (`0°`), ¾ front left/right (`30°–45°`), side profile (`90°`), ¾ rear (`150° / 210°`), rear (`180°`), opposite side (`270° / 330°`), top (`top-down`) and 1–3 details. When synthesizing or calibrating consistent multi-view boards from 1 cropped frontal image (on a square canvas `>= 320×320 px` with the object centered and size-normalized), use **fixed FOV of `30°`** (avoids wide-angle distortion in product close-ups), relative azimuths `30°, 90°, 150°, 210°, 270°, 330°` with alternating elevations `20°` and `-10°`, and passes of normal map (`view-space normal map`) + `depth map` for alpha mask extraction with edge accuracy above 98%. **Master** = ¾ front with the best light and sharpness.
2. **Standard package (individual + total):** real references saved at 2048 px on the longer side (`_ref`). The 360 has 6 fixed views, each saved as an individual file `vista_[angle]` and gathered in the total board `turnaround_[produto].jpg` at 3:2, grid of 3 rows × 2 columns with thin white dividers: ¾ front right | ¾ front left / right side | left side / front | back. A new board or view only replaces the one in the package after approval (`delivery.md` §2, proof 4). Same uniform light gray background, same light, scale and horizon height in all cells; whole product with breathing room and soft contact shadow. The board saved in the package has **no text** (caption in `PRODUTO_*.md`); the approval preview carries a caption per cell (view name + `real` or `gerada`), because without it the user does not know which angle is being judged. ¾ rear, top view and details are extras, outside the board. A generated view enters marked `_gerada`; real cutouts with alpha are the best source.
3. **Generating a missing view and Apply/Replace Mode (`Apply/Replace`):**
   - After the local and official consultation, generate the missing angles from the master + best available view, in a generator that accepts image references (`Product turnaround view: same product as supplied references seen from <angle>, neutral studio background, consistent proportions and identity`). Apply U12: compare observable traits with the safe sources, record what was inferred, correct deviations and present the board for approval through the creative cycle (`delivery.md` §2). Views are born in `Imagens\IA\` as `_gerada`, with status under evaluation; only after approval are they copied to `Referências\` as lower-weight synthetic support. They never replace an original photo nor become a real master; the provisional master exception of §1 remains identified as generated.
   - **Option for a view without a real photo that distorts** (offer to the user, not a mandatory step): lock the geometry before painting. Locally assemble a simple 3D model of the product (measurements from the datasheet + shape of the local references), render the views with the same camera, light and neutral background and use each render as the generator's **initial image** (img2img, partial noise ~0.8), not just as a reference; paint and details come from real cutouts. In the model, draw each opening (window, door, windshield) only where it exists on the product, so the generator does not read an extra part. If there is no time to model, the mesh can come from image-to-3D (Meshy, Rodin, Sparc3D, local Hunyuan3D) as **untextured clay**, for geometry only. For each view: first find the zero camera (the exact angle of the source photo), render the clay resting on the floor with cast shadow and paint that layout with **two** references, `[layout, source]`. Painting each angle from scratch, without a layout, drifts the scale by 7% to 53% between views, and clay over a flat photo floats. Do not run a "harmonize" pass that crops again, because it breaks the alignment. Regenerate only the rejected view. Check on a contact sheet: extra limb or part, swapped side, leftover gray clay, missing shadow.
   - To replace a product in an already approved scene without changing the environment (`Replace Mode`): `"Apply the reference product to the image and remove traces of any other product in the same category. Preserve the original environment and lighting intact; maintain the exact shape, proportions, materials, colors, brand identity and geometry of the reference product with realistic contact shadows"`.
4. **Identity checklist + Corrective rules + Descriptor (EN):** number the observable traits in `PRODUTO_[NOME].md` and separate in its own field the inferences from generated views, with origin and approval status; an approved inference does not become proven fact. Observable checklist (silhouette, number/shape of windows or openings, position of engines/wheels/cap, exact stripes and colors, gloss/matte finish, forbidden items), add short rules for each detected error. Before generating a view without a photo, record the packaging construction: label type (partial adhesive, 360° sleeve, direct print), material, color and transparency. The unphotographed area follows that record; white or generic neutral is also invention and makes the view illegible. Label: the priority is always the real label, the original's artwork applied on top (recipe of §3). If it still fails repeatedly, look for another path, for example having the generator write the text: transcribe literally the legible text from the safe references in `PRODUTO_*.md`, pass it in quotes in the prompt and check the output letter by letter against the reference. An illegible stretch stays `[ilegível]`, never completed. Close with the descriptor in English + `"keep the object's shape, proportions, livery and details exactly as in the reference images; no added text beyond the quoted label, no registration marks"`.

### 2.0 Board variants (when the use calls for it)
The standard board of item 2 remains the base. Variants are assemblies of the same package, one call per cell (U12):
- **Six axes:** front, back, left, right, top and bottom, with equal perspective and distance. Serves packaging, technical piece and manual, which need the top and bottom.
- **3×3 grid of the same instant:** front, ¾ left, ¾ right, left, right, back, high angle, low angle and Dutch angle. Changes only camera and framing; identity, light, background and color stay fixed. Forbid camera, tripod or viewfinder in the frame; a blank grid used as a guide must not leak lines into the image.
- **Orbit ring:** 16 cameras at eye level (22.5° apart) + top, for a viewer or to serve as keyframes of the spin (§4).
- **Top view with photographer's position marked:** to plan a scene or shoot from a reference photo.

### 2.1 Product with interior (aircraft, vehicle, vessel, property, store, stand)
Inventory and record **exterior and interior** in the same `PRODUTO_*.md`, section `## Interior`:
- Look for interior photos in the client's archive (cabin, cockpit, seats, panel, rooms, inner facade) and identify which specimen/version they belong to; doubt = `_A_CONFIRMAR`, never assume.
- Record per configuration (e.g. executive × air ambulance, standard × luxury) with: files `interior_[config]_ref`, observable interior checklist (number and layout of seats, upholstery color/material, shape of windows seen from inside, lighting, floor/ceiling, equipment) and use vetoes (e.g. medical equipment only in pieces of that service).
- Interior generated only from the interior references of that specimen; the exterior does not define the interior. Without a real interior photo: record `Interior: sem referência — não gerar` and ask for the photos.

### 3. Execution and Verification
- **The 360 package is generator input, never a piece nor a cutout.** The final image is born **whole** (product + scene + light + shadow) in a generator that accepts image reference: attach **master + view of the requested angle + turnaround (+ 1 detail)** (3–4 images) and paste into the prompt the descriptor and checklist from `PRODUTO_*.md`; the scene follows Part C of `image.md` (visual argument, direction sheet, advertising physics and 3 internal directions before the first generation).
- **Forbidden as a substitute:** generating an empty setting and pasting the cutout (Hybrid Route), or putting the cutout/turnaround straight into the piece on a plain background. Perspective, light and scale do not match and the piece becomes a catalog, not advertising.
- **Gate before spending:** check whether the session's generator accepts a reference image. Text only, unlinked account or no key = **stop**, say in 1 line what is missing and what unlocks it (link account, API key, run `generate_image.py generate ... --ref` (generates, upscales and registers image) on the user's PC) and offer the honest alternative (the client's real photo with advertising treatment). Never degrade silently nor generate an empty scene "to compose later".
- **Verified recipe for a scene with several products (kit):** (1) strong generator with several references, in the order Codex/ChatGPT (`ia_parceira.codex`), Gemini with quota and only then the 4B local engine, which serves as a composition draft; `local_engine.py stop` (analyzes the PC and installs the local generator) when done, because stuck RAM brings Codex down. (2) One call with 3 images: approved scene composition, official renders of the products in scene order and front art of the labels (PDF panel). The generator ignores scale requested in text: give the % of width, check and, if wrong, ask only for the scale adjustment with the background extended. (3) Label: the real PDF art (or the official render with alpha) goes on top at final resolution, with the bottle's shadow preserved (median filter over the area, without the ink), cylinder curvature at the edges and scale by height; never trust the generated text. (4) Resolution: refine by product crops with ~50 px of context, one bottle or pair per call, and only then reapply the label, because refinement rewrites the text (measured: refinement rewrites label words). Stitching between crops and per-crop scale record still to be validated.
- Check item by item of the checklist against the master; fix the cause of the deviation, improve/swap references, edit or regenerate as the case may be. Continue while there is progress and preserve fidelity; stop if the tool blocks or new attempts do not improve the result. Two attempts without improvement = measure the output before the third (`delivery.md` §2, proof 3).

### 4. Product Spin 360°, exploded view and cutaway (motion deliverables)
Deliverables distinct from the board, which is a prerequisite: without an approved package (§2), the spin invents the side nobody saw. Output in `Imagens\IA\[Produto]\` or `Vídeos\`; assembly, captions and soundtrack follow `video.md`.

**Route by input (the first that fits):**
1. **Client 3D model or approved clay (.glb/.gltf/.obj/.blend) → render in Blender**, local and free, because it is deterministic and does not deform the product. Prepare: an AI-derived mesh loses the normal and roughness maps (they produce speckled reflection) and receives smooth values; studio light in 3 to 4 areas (key, fill, rim, bounce), warm color in the light and not in the world, weak 0.95 gray world. Find the front with 4 test frames (front, right, back, left) and confirm with the user if in doubt. Camera aiming at an empty at the center of the object, rotating the empty; radius, height and lens fixed (50 mm) and no keyframe. Perfect loop: linear interpolation and the 360° keyframe one frame after the last rendered one (frame N+1), otherwise the spin stutters at the seam. Default 5 s at 24 fps (120 frames), transparent film; export ProRes 4444 or WebM with alpha, MP4 for social networks and PNG sequence for a viewer. In a long render, count the frames in the folder before encoding.
   - **1b. Product without mesh, with reliable frontal source → 3D reconstructed from the source.** **Shape:** measure from the cleanest source with sub-pixel precision and correct only what is source distortion (shadow, cutout noise and perspective: with a high or low camera, rounded bottom and top appear as an ellipse and the silhouette there is not a profile; separate shape and angle before using, and the shape must stand on its own), without swapping measured curves for ideal shapes; pieces and seams without steps, gaps or folds the original does not have, with the curve leaving tangent from the straight part and without inverting the curvature (label and sticker copy any dent). **Artwork:** the highest-quality one, applied at the file's resolution and color, as an opaque material; uniform scale, cropped only at the margin and never compressed or stretched. Label or sticker sits on the straight area of the packaging, centered between the start of the curves; calibration against the original adjusts scale and content, because the position measured in a render with perspective shifts and leaves a loose seam. Render above the output size and downscale with a quality filter (supersampling), and compress high without color subsampling, because 3D smoothing does not smooth text inside the texture and compression blurs fine lettering. **Calibrate by measuring, not assuming:** scale, color and shape checked against the original in a calibration view, with numbers, until the difference is within a few percent. **Realism:** light (including the bounce that lightens the lower faces), reflection, texture and physical details (reliefs, edges, material shine) follow the original in shape and tone, in balanced dose. Render in Blender or local Three.js.
2. **Photos only or 360 package → video in a generator with reference** (image-to-video or reference-to-video): attach master + turnaround and do not describe the product again in the prompt, only camera and light, because redescribing competes with the reference. Product almost still and a list of invariants (silhouette, colors, label in quotes, named parts). Timed script: hook up to 2 s, hero spin, detail macro every 5–7 s (a pure spin loses attention at around 5 s), pause on the hero ¾ and wrap-up. Movements: continuous orbit, half turn of 180° or 90°, ±45° pendulum, high-angle spin, crane rise, 180° flip on the long axis with a colored light band sweeping across the piece.
3. **Interactive store viewer → frame sequence** (24 to 36 per turn, or the ring of 16 from §2.0), same camera, light and scale in all, assembled in HTML through the `html.md` route, with a static fallback image.

**Formats:** 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16; constant 24 or 30 fps; product page 8–15 s, social 15–30 s; product filling 50–80% of the frame, never below 40%; text with contrast ≥ 3:1. Known limits: Amazon H.264 ~5 Mbps up to 500 MB; Shopify up to 100 MB. Check the platform's current specification before exporting.

**Exploded view (`Exploded View`):** parts move apart slowly, orbit with parallax and rim light tracing the seams, parts return to fit. Geometry, scale and finish fixed; reject duplicated part, part that crosses another, shaking and deformation. With a 3D model, Blender moves the parts apart via their own meshes, which guarantees the real component count. **Cutaway (`Cross-Section`):** only with the internal structure confirmed in the datasheet, photo or client model; invented interior does not enter, same as §2.1.

**Motion verification:** check frames at ~2 per second (interval = fps ÷ 2), part by part, per U12; the label is read back letter by letter on the most frontal frame. Typical defects: shaking in the spin (inconsistent fps; check at full zoom), light that changes between stitched photos (normalize or use a dissolve), duplicated eyelet or part, blur and flicker. Rejected: regenerate from the package with the invariant tightened, never from the derived video.

**Engines (options, per U12 and `operations.md` §0.6):** local Blender is the preferred route whenever there is a mesh. For video from photos, external platforms (Higgsfield/Seedance, Kling, Runway, Scenario and similar) enter the list when the session has access, through the user's account. None available or all failed = Failure Rule; the question includes installing a free local engine, offered only if `local_engine.py detect` approves the hardware, with the one-time approval before any download.

### 5. Product concept photo (`/product-concept` and `/input` with a product)
Deliverable distinct from the packshot and the lookbook: the product serves an idea, creates desire and atmosphere, and lives physically inside the scene (never pasted flat). Uses the packaging sources and priorities of §1 (art PDF > official render > photo), the gate of §3 and the approval and redo cycle per slot below (same pattern as `/360-product`).

0. **Inventory of the input and the N products (before generating):**
   - List all products sent or cited (folders, names or photos): **they define the exact count (`1..N`) of the scope**, and only the user can exclude an item.
   - For each product `1..N`, inventory its folder in `Originais\Produtos\[linha]\[item]\` (vector art in PDF, sequence of 360/angle renders and package in `_DESIGN\Produtos\`). If there are already official renders + PDF in the client's folder, they **are already the 360 package of that item** (do not recreate from scratch); only run through `/360-product` first the item that has no source at the needed angle.
   - **`/input` mode (concept image sent as base):** the image becomes the master scene and locks setting, light direction, atmosphere, physics and scale, except what the user lists to change (e.g. background color). If the base image has fewer or more products than the requested `N` (e.g. 3 bottles in the sent scene, but 4 products requested), adapt the spatial slots in the same scene to accommodate exactly the `N` products in their real relative proportions (e.g. 10 ml ampoules smaller than 20/30 ml bottles). Without a sent image, follow steps 1–2 to create the concept from scratch.
1. **Study (when creating a concept from scratch):** (a) product, use, material and natural world; (b) `_DESIGN\guia.md`, `design_system.md` §§3–4 and what was already generated: `[CONCEITO]` lines of `historico.md` and, while they do not exist, names and `.txt` of `Imagens\IA\`; (c) each client reference becomes a card per `ads.md` §2.2 and §2.4.
2. **Choose the concept (only without `/input` or if a new concept is requested):** raise 6+ candidates (`references/concept/library.md` §§1–2), each in one sentence "product + idea through a visual relationship", filtering by the product's physical function in the scene, immediate beauty and anti-repetition (change ≥ 3 of 4 axes relative to the history). Generate the winner and cite 2 reserves in 1 line.
3. **Execution (kit or `/input` with N products), validated kit recipe.** Forbidden to deliver a deformed product or AI-written label when there is an official render/PDF: generators hallucinate small typography and swap colors and caps in scenes with several bottles. Also forbidden to generate an empty scene to paste a cutout (§3).
   - **(a) Generation:** Codex (`ia_parceira.codex`), then Gemini; the 4B local engine only does a composition draft and failed in Input with a product (failed in scene test). One call with 3 images: master scene (the one sent in `/input` or the approved one), official renders of the `N` products in slot order and front art of the labels (PDF panel). The prompt locks camera, background, glass or water and light; describes each slot with shape, cap and color; and gives the % of width the group occupies.
   - **(b) Scale:** the generator ignores scale requested in text. Measure the real % and, if wrong, ask only for the scale adjustment, with the background extended and the products untouched.
   - **(c) Resolution:** refine by crops (one bottle or pair per call, ~50 px of context) and upscale 2×.
   - **(d) Real label:** never the one written by the generator. At the end, the PDF art goes on top of each slot (2× supersampling + Lanczos, cylinder curvature or slot tilt, scale by height, bottle shadow preserved with median filter), with `assets\apply_label.py` (one slot per call: `--box`, `--body`, `--center`, `--radius`), because refinement rewrites the text. A tilted product or one inside water requires perspective per slot, not yet validated: measure at zoom before showing. From the scene only light, reflection and contact shadow go on top; never cover, distort or blur a letter.
4. **Approval Method in `/360-product` style (Board per Slot `1..N`):**
   - Before showing the piece, assemble in `Trabalho\prancha_conferencia_[titulo].jpg` the comparison board containing one row/column for each of the `N` products: `[Slot # and Name] | [Official Source: Render + PDF] | [1:1 Crop in the Final Scene]`, checking shape, cap, content color and letter-by-letter reading (target ≥ 95% fidelity).
   - Save the generated scene in `Imagens\IA\[Linha_ou_Produto]\concept\` with full `.txt` and place in `Aprovação\` the scene preview together with the board/numbered caption of the slots (`1..N`) so the user can approve the set or point to only the slot they want adjusted.
5. **Isolated redo per slot (`/360-product` cycle, `delivery.md` §2):**
   - Checklist in `PRODUTO_*.md` or `Trabalho\checklist.md`: (1) all `N` requested products present in the correct proportion; (2) geometry, cap and color identical to the official render; (3) label 100% legible letter by letter against the PDF; (4) credible physical integration (light, refraction, contact) without the look of a flat collage.
   - **A product failed (by the check or by the user):** never regenerate the whole scene nor touch the already approved products; go back to the 360 package/original **of the rejected slot only**, fix the cause (tilt, scale, light/water mask or PDF application) and replace only that slot. Approved: record in `historico.md` (`[CONCEITO] produto: ideia | resultado | caminho`) and the recipe in `guia.md`.
