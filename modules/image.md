### 2. How the Machine Learns (Evolving Memory and the "Edit Instead of Re-roll" Rule)
1. **Reading past performance:** before generating, read `## 2. O QUE FAZER` in `_DESIGN\design_system.md` (and `PRODUTO_*.md` / `PERSONAGEM_*.md`) to use the engine and prompt structure already approved for the client.
2. **Iteration and recovery:** the same part came out wrong in two consecutive generations (view, piece, side, detail; proof 3 of `delivery.md` §2) = stop and ask the user for a real reference of that part, and trigger the technical research of `image-product.md` §1; without a reference the generator invents, and more attempts only waste time. If an engine is not available, try the next compatible **generator** (2 → 2b → 2c → 2d → 3; 2d and 3 enter silently only with a recorded approval, otherwise they become the single question of `operations.md` §0.6); none available = Failure Rule (`operations.md` §0.6) + Manual Mode; option Z only as a last resort and with a warning. If the image does not work, use the visual feedback to decide between fixing the direction, editing the existing image, changing engine, composing assets or redesigning the scene. Edit preserving identity/facts that must not change; do a new generation when that is better for the idea. Do not force a fixed sequence/ceiling of mutations nor persist in calls that do not improve the result.
3. **Learning record:** every output (including product scene) in `Compartilhada\Imagens\IA\AAAA-MM-DD_[request]_[theme]_v[n].png`; a rejected one goes, with the `.txt` and the rejection reason, to `IA\Não aprovadas\`, leaving in `IA\` only the approved one and what is under evaluation — never in `Originais\`, `Banco de Imagens\` or product/character `Referências\` (`generate_image.py` refuses these destinations) — generate a `.txt` with `ferramenta`, `status`, `referencias`, `notas` and `PROMPT`. On approval, record the winning recipe in 1 line in `design_system.md`.

## Part B · Prompt Engineering, Hallucination Prevention, Commercial Photography, Physical Manipulation and Vision Decomposer

### 1. Prompt by intent, engine and scene
- Start from the visual idea and the action/relationship the image must express; choose language, prose, slots or JSON according to the engine and complexity. There is no mandatory verb, language, subject/environment proportion or prompt template.
- **Precision when needed:** clarify relative position when there is risk of spatial ambiguity, counts when the number is relevant, and entity limits when the model is losing elements. Simplify or split a scene only if that improves the result; dense scenes and deliberately complex compositions are not forbidden.
- **Identity and facts:** preserve the requested product, person, brand and details; text/logo/label that must be exact must be applied as real assets outside the generated image. Metaphors and abstractions are welcome when not presented as factual evidence.
- Use `avoid`/`negative_prompt` when the engine offers that channel and there is something concrete to exclude; do not discard the model's natural approach for a fixed blacklist.
- **Background to receive later composition:** if the image needs text/logo space, describe the scene and the breathing-room areas visually. Avoid instructions that ask the generator to draw the real text, CTA or badges; spatial guidance can be positive and integrated into the scene.
- **Structured batch:** use JSONL or slots only when the user asks for batch/structuring or when the tool requires it. Define only fields useful to the task; do not impose a full technical schema on a single image.
- **Quick modifiers:** `/input` non-negotiable reference (local engine: the image enters via `--ref`, first; Easy Diffusion: `--init` with `--strength 0.25`) · `/pullout` wide shot · `/wormsview` low-angle (worm's-eye) shot · `/floatingproduct` levitating product · `/8k` `/besttexture` in the finish · `/promptmaster` (lean block derived on the spot for external use).
- Generators do not deliver a transparent background: generate on a plain background and remove it, or compose in the SVG (Hybrid Route). Background prompt without brand name.
- Batch: list the concepts in PT and approve the set before generating the `.jsonl` (1 job per line).

### 2. Commercial Photography Direction, Optics and Material Physics
- **Perspective and framing before lens:** first describe the perceived relationship — close/intimate, wide/environmental, compressed/serene, low/imposing, frontal/documentary — and the needed crop. Focal length is a useful approximation when it changes perspective or framing; do not assign an exact lens to an image without metadata nor stack lens, aperture, sensor, ISO and shutter as prompt decoration. The same lens language can produce different results across engines.
- **Depth of field:** define what must remain legible and which planes tell the story. Use focus/blur to separate, bring closer or contextualize; do not apply extreme bokeh as a synonym of cinematic. Preserve geometry and detail when product, physical text or visual evidence must be recognized.
- **Motivated lighting:** determine apparent source, direction, size/quality, contrast, falloff and reflections that serve the scene; check that shadows and surfaces respond to that same source. Key/fill/rim are an analysis model, not an obligation of three lights. Specify contrast ratio, Kelvin or studio setup only when provided, measured or materially altering the result. Dramatic light and teal-orange grade are not defaults.
- **Reference vocabulary (not a preset):** when the requested effect has a name, use the term for its function, not its prestige. Light: soft (calm, commercial), hard (graphic, fashion), backlight (separation, edge), rim (glass and metal outline), practical (visible lamp, screen or candle), high key (clean, beauty, e-commerce), low key (suspense). Focal length as perspective: 14–20 mm scale and distortion; 24–28 mm environmental realism; 35 mm natural perspective; 50 mm balanced portrait and product; 85 mm compressed portrait and isolation; 100 mm macro product, jewelry, eye detail. Focus: shallow isolates the hero, deep keeps the environment legible, rack focus moves attention between two points. Grade: neutral (commercial, architecture), warm (comfort, hospitality), cool shadows (technology, night), desaturated (documentary), high-contrast monochrome (noir, fashion), pastel (beauty, wellness). Texture: fine grain (organic), clean digital (luxury, technology), halation and mist filter (soft glow, romance), high shutter speed (sharp action), motion blur (speed). A term with no function in the piece stays out.
- **Skin and representation:** keep plausible texture, expression and skin tones, with no gray, clipping, lightening or unintended beautification. The scene's color intent does not authorize distorting the identity of real people.
- **Surface and Material Physics:** specify real physical behavior — diffuse reflection, specular roughness, anisotropy (brushed aluminum/steel), *subsurface scattering* (human skin, amber liquids, leaves, wax) and refractive index with real caustics (thick glass, ice, water).

### 3. Advertising Manipulation, 3D Packshot and Physical Integration (Composites & Impossible Photography)
When inserting a real product into a generated scene or creating surreal/impossible concepts (e.g. monumental-scale product in the city), **local physics is non-negotiable**:
1. **Perspective and Horizon:** align camera height, horizon line and vanishing points between product and setting.
2. **Contact and grounding:** choose the physically visible signals relevant to the scene — contact shadow, cast shadow, occlusion, reflection or color bounce. Intensity, sharpness and extent depend on scale, surface, light source and resolution; do not demand a package of three effects nor a universal pixel value.
3. **Fresnel Reflections and Environmental Response:** on polished or wet surfaces, apply reflection with *Fresnel falloff* (stronger at grazing angles) and real physical interaction (drops, dust, mist or reflection of the surroundings on the product, never covering or deforming the label/logo).

### 4. Corporate Portrait, Wardrobe, 4K Upscale and Panter Mode (Photo Recovery)
- **Strict facial preservation (anti-beautification):** keep eyes, expression, wrinkles, micropores and facial hair 100% authentic. `soft skin`, plasticized skin, rejuvenation or enabling CodeFormer (`--face`) on a real client photo are forbidden.
- **Wardrobe and neutral screens:** when requested, adapt to contemporary tailoring with visible weave; laptops/phones always generic and without third-party logos without authorization. In posters with multiple speakers, test hierarchies and group compositions that favor identification and reading; triangle is an option, not a preset.
- **Recovery of washed-out/gray photo:** compare with the original and the KV; recover contrast, tonal separation and color only where the image data and requested direction call for it. Do not impose a saturation threshold, color percentage, accent block, warm/cool contrast or new light without basis in the source or the request.
- **Low-resolution input or compressed screenshot (AI Restoration and Faithful Copy):**
  1. *Warning and Non-Destructive Improvement:* first, look for the larger original in the client folders and at the source (`delivery.md` §2, proof 2); if none exists, issue a low-quality warning. Try first local tools that improve without altering the original (deterministic 2×/4× upscale via `assets\generate_image.py upscale` or `assets\treat_image.mjs`).
  2. *Authorization for AI:* if the image is still not sharp enough for the output format, ask whether the AI may generate an exact higher-quality AI copy.
  3. *1:1 Fidelity Prompt:* use `img2img` with low noise (0.25–0.35) or an image reference with a strict prompt: `"Ultra-high definition remastered photograph, exact identical 1:1 replica of the supplied reference. Pristine razor-sharp focus, 8k studio resolution, zero compression artifacts, zero grain. Strictly preserve original geometry, exact proportions, material textures, reflections, colors and literal label typography. Clean neutral lighting, master quality."`
  4. *Two attempts without improvement (Updated Prompt Search):* if the generation does not improve in two attempts, search GitHub on the spot, via the internet, for the best updated community prompt for the specific case (e.g. photorealistic remastering/product turnaround repositories and discussions) and adapt it.
  5. *Repeated failure after search:* if two new attempts do not improve even after updating the prompt with the GitHub search, stop immediately and ask via the question tool whether the user prefers to keep trying or explore other options (e.g. vectorization, manual collage in SVG, real photography).
  6. *Organization and Preservation:* originals are never replaced (Law 5). Save the generated image in `Compartilhada\Imagens\IA\`. If it is a specific product or character, organize it in its own subfolder named after the item: `Compartilhada\Imagens\IA\[Product or Character Name]\`.

### 5. Vision Decomposer (Reverse Engineering Image → Prompt)
When the user attaches any image asking to extract the prompt, clone the aesthetic direction or do style transfer, run in **2 strict steps**:
1. **Block 1 — Optical Decomposition in 4 Axes (1–2 sentences each):**
   - `Subject & Blocking`: framing (ELS/MS/CU/ECU), power positioning, visual weight, clothing/materials, gaze direction;
   - `Environment & 3-Plane Depth`: separation into Foreground / Midground / Background, figure-ground relationship and aerial/tonal perspective;
   - `Lighting & Chiaroscuro`: light/shadow ratio, Key/Fill/Rim, broken light (gobos/foliage) and color reflections on skin/object;
   - `Tech, Optics & Film`: visible angle, perspective, depth of field and texture; focal length, filter or format only as an effect approximation, never as metadata inferred with certainty.
2. **Block 2 — Prompt Synthesis (immutable order in English, zero filler such as *"The image shows..."*):**
   `[Shot type, optics and angle], [Subject, blocking, visual weight, clothing and action], [Multi-plane environment (Foreground / Midground / Background), overlapping and geometry], [Lighting scheme, optical contrast, gobos and reflexes], [Color palette, temperature contrast and aerial perspective], [Color Grading, diffusion filters, film stock, textural artifacts]`

### 6. Pass³ Quality Test (10 seconds before showing any image)
1. **Pass 1 — Physics (2 s):** do gravity, horizon/perspective, shadow direction and reflections match the light source?
2. **Pass 2 — Detail (5 s):** hands with 5 natural fingers, real skin pores, clean edges without halo and zero scrambled text/logo? Product inside a scene: do the product's contact, shadow, scale and light come from the same source as the scene?
3. **Pass 3 — Brand (3 s):** do light temperature, `#HEX` palette and atmosphere belong to the client's KV?
Failed any Pass → mutate only the responsible layer and regenerate before showing.

## Part C · Advertising Image (Scene Direction and Hero Shot)
An ad image must sustain the commercial argument. Receive from `ads.md` §1 the **Visual Proposition**, the asset's function and the copy link the scene will show; without them, define them before curation or the prompt. This part also applies when the final image is an already existing real photograph: select the framing, gesture, gaze, light, depth and negative space that express the intent; do not treat "has the right person" as sufficient direction. Apply U9: distinguish context from factual proof; an elegant scene that only suggests the sector or technology fails. Without a confirmed differentiator, use context already present in the briefing/KV; never invent an attribute, result or claim. If the scene would serve any competitor equally when the brand is swapped, or only shows the product with no relation to the copy, redo the direction before generating.
**Visual direction without a technical briefing:** the user decides any axis they specify. On free axes, apply in this order: visual references of this request (`ads.md` §§2.2 and 2.3) → image recipe approved in `_DESIGN\design_system.md` → initial base below. KV and product/person fidelity limit all sources. Do not ask for camera, light, depth or atmosphere just because a reference was missing.
**Adaptable initial base:** the idea can be narrative, symbolic, editorial, surreal or documentary; choose what best turns the message into an image without treating metaphor as proof. Camera, light, scale, perspective, planes and atmosphere are direction decisions, not a sheet that demands all resources in every image. In any new creative that calls for impact — including executive, educational or quote content — actively consider the advertising/heroic/cinematic language and compare it with more restrained alternatives; do not reserve it for product campaigns. Do not impose sunset, wet floor, bokeh, mist, lens or cinematic palette as a preset, nor reject these resources when the scene justifies them. After approval, record the winning combination as the client's recipe (`delivery.md` §7); before that it is a creative hypothesis, not a proven standard.
1. **Direction sheet (1 line each, in the `.txt`):** role in the piece (cover, proof, endcard) · argument and visual proof · territory (`ads.md` §1) · hero gesture · framing/perspective · motivated light and contrast · depth/atmosphere · KV palette · breathing-room area if text comes later. Each choice must support the argument; "cinematic", "premium" and lens names alone are not direction.
2. **Hero angle by category (reference, not preset):** vehicle/aircraft may call for a low ¾ to show form and scale; tabletop product, ¾ or macro to reveal material/use; architecture, corrected perspective that shows livable space; person, distance and gaze that sustain the action; interior, POV of the user. Choose time, weather, reflection and light by the benefit and the KV. Sunset, wet floor, rim light and bokeh only come in when they have narrative or physical function.
3. **Hero hierarchy:** image, typography or symbol can be the protagonists. When a person or product is the hero, size and crop according to the concept and identity; avoid accidental crops that hinder recognition, without imposing an area percentage or full silhouette. The background may deliberately contrast or compete when that makes up the idea. In a real portrait, prefer a crop that preserves expression and gesture pertinent to the message; color treatment must respect existing skin and light, not apply generic toning or halo as a brand shortcut.
4. **Physical coherence:** check horizon, shadows, reflections, materials and light when they are visible and relevant; do not add contact shadow, reflective floor or rim light to scenes that do not need them.
5. **New concept:** use the creative hypothesis already chosen in `ads.md` §1 when that flow is active. For a single image, compare ideas lightly before generating; concept variety is worth more than three nearly identical renders. Show alternatives only when the user asks.
6. **Pass³ + advertising test:** check details that actually appear (identity, hands, accidental text, outline, perspective) and whether the image delivers the visual intent without distorting the brand or asserting facts without basis. It is not mandatory that every image be understood in 1 s, have a dominant photographic hero or reserve space for copy.
7. **Series:** when the image is part of a campaign/carousel, apply U10 in the direction sheet and compare the scenes on the board; keep time and environmental logic coherent, with a distinct composition for each message.

## Part D · Image Reading and Analysis
Use to describe an image, answer questions about what appears, extract legible text, compare images or understand visual elements. It is analysis, not generation or redesign.

1. **Start from what is available:** examine accessible attachments/images with the session's native multimodal vision capability. Do not require installing a skill, script, SDK, key, endpoint or external account to begin. A third-party skill or vision service may complement, but must never block a reading the current model can do.
2. **Route by intent without duplicating specialists:** brand creative calls for evaluation and evidence in `ads.md` §6; style/KV reference follows `ads.md` §2; visual prompt extraction uses the optional decomposition of Part B §5. This Part D covers general reading, description, questions, visual OCR and comparison. Do not force the reverse-prompt structure onto the other analyses.
3. **Observe and qualify:** separate what is clearly visible, what is inference and what cannot be confirmed from the image. Describe relationships, actions, objects, composition and context relevant to the question; do not infer EXIF, intent, identity, demographics, brand, commercial facts or illegible text. Quantity, subtle color and small detail may be uncertain; say so and do not fill gaps by plausibility.
4. **Text and detail:** transcribe only what you can read. Preserve spelling and order, mark uncertain fragments instead of completing words. Zoom/crop the region or use available OCR when it helps; use coordinates and confidence only if the tool actually provides them. For decisive price, date, claim, label, logo or legal text, confirm in the original or in an authorized text source when available. Vision does not promise pixel-perfect measurement: for exact position/font/alignment, prefer SVG/DOM/source file or appropriate technical inspection.
5. **Comparison and multiple images:** compare only the requested aspects, citing observable differences and without presuming which version is better. Check the images at a scale/detail compatible with the content; a board can help with the general relationship, but does not replace zoom for small text. If the visual analysis fails or the image is not accessible, report the limitation and ask for a legible file/crop or an accessible visual input; never write findings as if you had seen the image.
6. **Structured resources are optional:** regional captions, tags, detection with boxes, OCR with confidence/polygons and crop suggestions can be useful in automation or localized inspection when an already authorized integration is available. Do not install the Azure SDK, ask for credentials or send client images to external services automatically; this requires explicit choice and authorization. Do not create an API dependency for an ordinary analysis. Use Azure Vision Python/Java only when the user asks for software implementation on that stack or when the existing integration is explicitly chosen; select only necessary features and report real authentication, limits and errors.


### Cinema as language, not preset
For a static key visual or poster, direct a moment, not a coverage sequence: what action/tension is happening, where the subject is in relation to the environment, what their gesture/gaze communicates and how perspective, scale, light and masses lead the eye to the meaning. Choose only the resources needed for reading and the KV; "cinematic" does not require a named lens, flare, mist, backlight, grain, teal-orange palette or blur. If the task calls for movement, multiple shots, coverage, temporal continuity or editing, route to `video.md`; do not convert editing rules into a checklist for an image.

## Part E · Consistent execution with references (product, person and character)
Applies to `image-product.md` and `image-character.md` (standard U12 of `standards.md`). Text moved from U12, with no rule change:

- Prompt = base identical byte for byte in every call (geometry, camera, scale, light, named parts, colors, text) + only the change in the last sentence, because "same as the previous one" conveys nothing.
- One call per view or expression, because a batch does not carry per-item change.
- Each view comes from the master/package, never from the previous output, because chaining adds up drift, and a seed does not convey identity.
- 2–4 references, each with a single declared role ("image N = angle only; do not copy light"), because a reference without a role lends its own errors.
- The board is an assembly of approved views, never a reference while there is a slot for loose views.
- In motion, check frames at ~2 per second, part by part: gaining, losing, recoloring or deforming a part fails; moving does not.
- **Several references, each with a role:** one sentence stating the medium (photo, illustration), then one line per image ("image N = WHO / WHERE / POSE / ANGLE / STYLE / LIGHT / PALETTE / WARDROBE / EXPRESSION; take nothing else"), and a framing line. The role the generator ignores most, usually style, goes first. An image that must not contribute gets "ignore completely". Check leakage per image: its role appears, and nothing beyond it. If wrong, rewrite that image's line, do not retouch. Two or more people in the same scene: each with their own package and a distinct WHO role; if the traits blend, generate each person separately and compose with matched light and perspective.
