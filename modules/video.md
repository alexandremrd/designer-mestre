# Video and Motion module · one studio (cut, motion, captions, voice, script and AI direction)

Routes `video`, `reel`, `motion`, `captions`, `voiceover`/`audio`. **One flow for everything that moves**: spoken video, motion without a presenter, captions on a finished video, UGC and AI video. What changes between requests is the **input profile** (§0), which switches steps on or off; the capabilities, gates and preview are the same. This file replaces the old `reel.md` and Part C of the video module; nothing from either was left out.

The cut engine and the animated-piece kit are third-party, copied **intact** into `engines\reel\` (credit in `NOTICE.md`; `S` = `engines\reel`). The user's decisions on where the engine yields to the rest of the skill are in `references\reel-conflicts.md` (C1 to C12) and prevail over the engine text.

## 2. Script, speech and cut
### 2.1 Script (P3, P4 and briefing without ready speech)
- **Format by commercial goal:** direct conversion or offer = presenter, avatar or direct-response UGC; visual product or lifestyle = cinematic B-roll + voiceover; social proof = 1st-person UGC; mixed test = hook with a person (0 to 3 s) + product B-roll in the body.
- **5-bullet arc (30 s, at most 80 spoken words):** HOOK 0 to 3 s (always generate 3 angles, **Pain**, **Curiosity** and **Direct offer**, and pick the strongest, because it must stop the scroll), PROBLEM 4 to 8 s, SOLUTION 9 to 18 s, PROOF 19 to 24 s (concrete result, confirmed number or demonstration), CTA 25 to 30 s (single, imperative action). For 15 s: 3 bullets, hook 0 to 3 s, solution and proof 4 to 11 s, CTA 12 to 15 s, up to 40 words. At most 2 spoken sentences per bullet; an A/B test changes **only** the first 3 seconds of the hook.
- **Dramaturgy before the prompt or the grid:** **Scene formula** in 1 sentence: `Desire + Obstacle + Spatial Geometry (Foreground / Midground / Background) + Gaze Direction + Rhythm`. **Law of 3 physical details per take:** environmental pressure (wind, steam, rain reflection, raking light), micro-action of the body or product, and a sound anchor or visual motif. Claims: no scene shows a guarantee, financial result, comparison or number without a confirmed source (Law 4); risk becomes 1 line. In an ad, clarity and rhythm come before color grading or elaborate transition.

### 2.2 Spoken text (narration and captions come from it)
- **When:** every motion or video with voice, already at the briefing analysis (`briefing.md` §3), before any scene; also when the user asks only for the voiceover text. **Sole goal:** extract from the briefing, script or copy what is **spoken**, as clean running text, ready for the voice generator. It is not a copy review nor a new script.
- **In:** speech, voiceover, voice-over, narration, dialogue and **spoken** CTA, in the order they happen. **Out:** scene and visual-direction notes, timings, on-screen text (lettering, cards, chips), camera instructions, visual CTA, ad copy or post caption, hashtags, credits and disclaimers.
- **Cleanup:** remove markers ("Scene 3", "Narration:", "VO:", character name before the line, direction brackets such as "[pause]"); one paragraph per speech block, no bullets, opening quotes or numbering. **Fidelity:** keep words, order and language variant (PT-BR, PT-PT, EN); touch only punctuation; pronunciation spelling (acronym, number, brand) only with the user's ok. Plans A/B/C with the same speech: extract once and warn; different speech: one text per plan, identified outside the text.
- **No speech in the material:** first use what exists elsewhere (another plan, script, caption); with none, derive it from the lettering or copy without adding information, mark it as created in the `.txt` and proceed only after approval. Created narration is speech: complete, natural sentences, questions with "?", correct accents and no symbol (`/ * @ #`) that is not a command for the AI.
- **Output:** show the text and write `Trabalho\narration.txt` (spoken only). Report words and estimated duration (~2.5 words per second in PT-BR) against the requested duration and the platform's rhythm (Stories ~15 s); if it exceeds, show alongside a condensed version, derived from the material and without new information, and generate the voice only after the choice, because long speech holds a still screen.

### 2.3 Cut and recorded speech (P1; conditional in P2 and P4)
**Cutting or assembling speech is creative editing**, not just removing silence; silence alone leaves the video long and rhythmless. Before cutting, build the plan over the transcript: §2.1 arc within the briefing's duration; **Law of the 3 Functions** (speech that does not change the emotion, advance the story or raise the pressure goes, even without silence around it); **Murch's Rule of Six** at the cut point (Emotion 51% > Story 23% > Rhythm 10% > Eye-trace 7% > 2D plane 5% > 3D space 4%): cut on the change of expression or gaze, never mid-gesture or mid-breath; **Montage Ladder** (`long > shorter > shorter > breathing pause > impact`; the camera moves only when the scene changes); a passage outside the briefing or forbidden goes (`--remove`); the plan's pauses stay whole (`--breath`); transitions outside speech, without overlapping phonemes. The user approves the cut together with the grid, in a short table (speech · keep or cut · why).
1. **Cut engine**, in order and inside `Trabalho\reel\cut\`: `1-takes.py` (take map by energy; read all of `takes.txt`) > choice of *keeps* (the best complete take of each sentence; false starts out) > `2-fillers.py` > `3-cut.py` (4-frame J/L cuts; GPU encode) > `4-review.py` with `--prompt "<proper names>"`. Acceptance (R5): all `review.txt` blocks approved, **no word eaten and no pause longer than 0.25 s**.
2. **Eaten word:** protect the interval from the raw (`raw`/`force`) and cut again. **Leftover pause:** shorten the protected interval. Check each seam by windowed transcription and listen to each seam: a visual fade does not fix truncated audio.
3. **Speech timing:** validate the start by audio energy. Whisper's per-word timing with `-dtw` comes out chained from 0.0 s and hides the pauses (uncut initial silence, early caption): use Whisper's speech segments, refined by energy, and distribute the words within them. Cut only on measured silence.
4. **Video that already went through another cut (P2):** `caption_reels.py` cuts pauses by default (`--cut-pauses 0.45 --slack 0.18`, leaving ~0.36 s of natural pause, the cut lands only on measured silence; also applies in a custom pipeline); the P1 cut engine is stricter (no pause longer than 0.25 s).
5. If the video is already clean, skip the cut: copy it to `cut\clean.mp4` and transcribe with `S\tools\prepare.py`. Outputs are `clean.mp4`, `cut.wav`, `edl.json` and `cut_words.json`.

## 5. Beat grid (a single map, approved before rendering)
Before animating, associate each stretch of speech with a visual action that moves the argument forward; choose framing, media, hierarchy and transition as parts of that action; use a compatible rhythm reference when there is one. The **grid** opens with the proposed **editing style** (§6.1) and **soundtrack** and is a table: **time · speech · camera or layout · piece · effect or sound · acceptance criterion** (and the beat, `60/BPM` s, if there is a soundtrack). It is the engine's `assets\map.md` and the per-scene card; it includes the **proposed soundtrack** and the spoken text. **Show it and wait for the user's yes (C2)**; after the yes, render without further stops. Before the grid, stop only for a missing fact, price or name. Planning and checking are internal; ask for a decision only for a material choice.

**Render engine is an option, not an obligation:** HyperFrames, Remotion and the HTML exporter do not switch off the flow's capabilities. The AI picks the best for the piece, including a superior one found on the internet or local, as long as (a) it states the observable gain in 1 line, (b) installs only in the resource root, (c) tests with one scene before assembling everything, (d) the rules of face, safe zone, level, real material and approved grid keep applying. Remotion: free for individuals and companies with up to 3 employees; above that it requires a Company License, confirm before production. Adobe Express (optional connector, `operations.md` §6) is an alternative to animate an approved design and resize video when it delivers better or faster; the rules above continue.
**Single contract of the animated page:** every animation page exposes `window.__render(t)` (positions the timeline at instant `t`, without playing audio; the same `t` produces the same frame, without depending on a transition in progress), `window.__DUR` (duration in seconds) and a `#stage` stage with the output size (the exporter puts it at scale 1). The same page serves `export_motion.py`, the template's transparent-background pieces and the preview.

**Component library** (one, three origins):
- **From the kit (`K.*`, ~65, approved in production):** layouts and camera (`layout`, `punch`, `inset`, `takeover`, `hideMouth`, `mouthCaps`), panels and screens (`panel`, `terminal`, `stylePanel`, `files`, `notes`, `dashbox`), lettering and pieces (`pill`, `ring`, `stamp`, `topTitle`, `behindTitle`, `kickerTop`, `price`, `cta`, `comment`, `category`), comparison (`versus`, `compare`, `beforeAfter`, `fan`, `grid`, `wall`, `stack`, `row3d`, `layers3d`, `orbit`, `explode`), flow (`flow`, `chain`, `progress`, `scrub`, `cursor`), effects (`glitch`, `flash`, `leak`, `drift`, `marquee`, `camcorder`, `faceHud`, `waveCut`), sound (`sfx`). Nothing generic: each one enters at the instant of the word.
- **Narration scenes (for P3 and gaps without a presenter):** `Opening`, `Anchoring`, `Benefit Block 1..N`, `Data/Number`, `Quote`, `Closing/CTA`, built as reusable modular components (**Comps**, the `Comps × Skin`) that receive the client's **Skin** through variables (`#HEX` colors, official type family, logo, sound signature).
- **Semantic segmentation of the copy:** short impactful question or statement = **Opening** (kinetic typography `KineticText`: giant words or short phrases, 30 to 45 frames at 30 fps, `fontSize` 110 to 150 px, `lineHeight` 1.05 to 1.1, background in soft zoom 1 to 1.2 with `opacity` 0.3); price or comparison = **Anchoring** (old value in the first 60 frames and an impact micro-transition (`interpolate([60, 65, 70], [-20, 20, 0])`) revealing the condition at 160 px, uppercase, KV accent color); number, `%` or `R$` = **Data**; quotation marks = **Quote**; imperative verb = **CTA** (background in the KV's action color, maximum contrast).
**Physics follows the engine:** in the kit, GSAP (`power3.out`, `power3.inOut`); in Remotion, spring (`spring`): title entrance `damping 12, stiffness 150, mass 0.5`; opacity and camera `damping 200`. Polished product: no `bounce`; playful: free (`quality.md` §C). Curves and recipes in `references\motion\` (HTML and CSS recipes first: `motion-cookbook.md`).
**Motion vocabulary** (a single one, with the context saying which applies): spoken vertical video: blur→sharp entrance 0.22 to 0.5 s `power3.out`, blur+fade exit 0.2 to 0.25 s, layout change 0.45 s `power3.inOut` (never a hard cut), punch-in 1.06 base and 1.14 to 1.20 at impact. Motion without presenter and interface: starting point 0.18 / 0.35 / 0.6 s, scale 0.95 to 1.04, stagger 0.05 to 0.10 s, exit at 60 to 70% of the entrance (`quality.md` §C). In all: **every piece enters at the word's onset** (anchored in the transcript, never in guessed seconds); something new every 1.5 to 3 s; internal panel steps word by word; caption of 1 to 3 words that follows the mouth; SFX only in big pieces; camcorder HUD hook followed by glitch; dark glass with the color in the graphic and not on the face. Stiffness or lack of dynamism is fixed in the **choreography** (entrance, sustain and exit, speed continuity, trajectory, relation to speech), not only in the easing; sustain movement enters when requested or when it serves, without the same oscillation on all layers.
**Face and safe zone:** the face is never covered (R1); lettering and pills on the chest, heavy content in split screen; title behind the head only if there are more than ~300 px free above it, otherwise lettering on the chest and the mouth caption hidden in that interval (L-4). Core safe zone (C4, U2): top 250, bottom 450 on Reels; nothing in the button column. Cropping by container or camera is not allowed; leaving the safe zone is.
**Density and reading:** set the rhythm by the speech, the reading and the function of each shot; highlights enter in the spoken stretch and stay long enough to be read; progress indicator follows the direction of the information (a countdown empties, it does not fill). **Alignment:** by the real outline of the text and the axis of the main image, not by the box; check at real size. **Render:** 1080×1350 (Feed) or 1080×1920 (Reels and Stories), official logo never distorted.

### 6.1 Editing style and captions
The user chooses the **editing style in the grid** (§5); with no choice: P1 and P3 come out in **Dynamic**, P2 in **Standard**. The caption is part of the style, but is not the style.
| Style | What it delivers | When | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dynamic** (the reel's) | caption of 1 to 3 words that **follows the mouth**, with **occasional keywords in the accent color**; **transitions** (animated layout change, punch-in, glitch); **element animation** (interface pieces anchored to the word); lean SFX | Reels, Shorts, ad, any video that needs rhythm and retention | modes A, B or C (§6): caption by `K.mouthCaps` (mode A) or `assemble_reels.py captions` with `highlighted_words` (mode B); the rest comes from the components and the motion vocabulary of §6 |
| **Standard** | caption in the KV + brand overlay + animated opening and closing, no interface pieces or punch | finished video, institutional, when the request is only to caption | `caption_reels.py` (§6.2) |
**Dynamic is not a more animated caption:** it is the whole set. A caption with accent color without pieces, transitions and element animation is just a standard caption; the 6 elements of §8 say what is missing to be dynamic.

**Standard caption variants** (`caption_reels.py --style`; options passed by hand, such as `--words`, `--max-chars`, `--size`, `--highlight` and `--fade`, override the variant):
| Variant | How it appears | What for |
|---|---|---|
| `standard` | 1 line, 1 to 3 words that follow the speech (`--words 3 --max-chars 24`), highlight in the accent color if `--highlight` | the common one |
| `stable` | short phrase on 1 line (up to ~34 characters, smaller body), no per-word highlight | explainer, longer video, calm reading |
| `caixa` | short phrase in a solid box in the outline color | very varied or cluttered background |
Two-line captions do not exist yet in the tools.

**Reading rules for any caption and any style:** the text is the **transcribed and checked speech** (never caption from the script without checking against the real speech; an existing `.srt` enters through `--srt`; otherwise local Whisper with GPU; a word corrected by the user = run again with `--srt`, without transcribing again); the font is the **exact KV family** (never fall to Arial silently, U3); the caption is the **topmost layer**, above overlay and gradient, legible over them, with the base at `y ≤ 1470` (default 1400), inside the safe zone, without covering face or logo; `--uppercase`, bold and italic only if the KV asks; no wrong word in the `.srt`. Animate only when it favors reading, without delaying the entrance or anticipating the exit; when in doubt, stable. The three caption tools (`caption_reels.py`, `assemble_reels.py` and `K.mouthCaps`) apply the same rule, each in its own mode; unifying their code is a recorded pending item.

### 6.2 Overlay, opening and closing (P2 and template)
- **Floor of a finished video:** animated opening H1 + caption in the KV + brand overlay + closing with approved H1 and CTA + 1080×1920 export. Opening, closing and overlay come from the client's approved template (or the last approved video), changing only the copy; create from scratch only what it does not cover. **Entrance, exit and CTA are always animated** (layers in sequence, ease-out, no bounce, soft exit of the text before the cut): the briefing's "no effects or transitions" restriction applies to cuts and scene effects, not to the entrance of the elements. Assemble the layers frame by frame, never by concatenating a still image in a loop (produces a gray first frame). With an approved template, reuse the client's `_DESIGN\templates\reels_motion\`.
- **Overlay:** soft entrances and exits for logo, frames, dividers and CTA, with acceleration and deceleration and a coherent sequence; preserve reading pauses, not everything in motion; check in playback, because a single frame does not validate fluidity. Overlay source: the client's Figma 9:16 frame exported as PNG 1080×1920 **with transparency** (`get_screenshot` does not preserve alpha; prefer a Figma export or assemble via SVG), or, with no frame, SVG 1080×1920 with the frame, gradient and logo from `design_system.md` rendered as RGBA PNG in the headless browser. The caption strip has a dark gradient **under** it, never over the face.

  `--y` is the caption **base** (in ASS `Alignment=2`, `MarginV = 1920 - y`). Each visual cut enters with a short cross dissolve (`--fade 0.25`), limited by the available silence; do not overlap words to meet a fixed duration. Other options: `--box` (solid box in the `--outline` color, `--border` = breathing room), `--remove A-B` (repeatable; removes the speech that starts between A and B s), `--breath T` (repeatable; keeps the pause that contains T), `--hook "line|line" --hook-s N` (text on the 1st frame, captions start afterward), `--endcard png --endcard-s 2.5` (enters with fade). The 1st run generates `<out>.srt`. No ffmpeg on PATH: `uv run --with imageio-ffmpeg --with faster-whisper python caption_reels.py ...`; transcription and encode try the GPU and fall back to the CPU on their own (U13). ### 6.3 AI scene direction and UGC (P4)
- **Camera vocabulary.** Movements: `Push-in` (tension, detail) · `Pull-back / Dolly out` (reveals context) · `Tracking shot` · `Pan` · `Tilt` (grandeur) · `Crane up/down` · `Orbit / Arc` (heroic spin on the product) · `Dolly zoom` (vertigo) · `Handheld / Steadicam` (UGC realism) · `Static / Locked-off` (clean packshot) · `Slow-motion` (luxury, impact) · `Timelapse`. Angles: `Eye-level` · `Low angle` (power, luxury) · `High angle` · `Bird's eye / Top-down` (flat-lay) · `Dutch angle` (tension) · `Over-the-shoulder / POV` (intimacy, unboxing).
- **The 7 pillars of UGC that doesn't look AI** (unboxing, review with a small natural hesitation, "Day with" routine, before and after with the same light and angle): (1) imperfect camera (`handheld smartphone footage, slight natural camera shake, selfie front-facing angle slightly below eye level, 1-3 degree tilt, casual reframing mid-shot`); (2) natural window light (`natural side window lighting, slight overexposure on one side, no ring light reflections, visible soft shadows`); (3) lived-in environment (`lived-in room background, everyday objects visible, no studio backdrop, faint natural ambient room noise`); (4) authentic performance (`authentic human expressions, natural micro-pauses before speaking, genuine smile building slowly, unscripted hand gestures, occasional glance down at product`); (5) smartphone optics (`shot on smartphone portrait mode, f/1.8 shallow bokeh, slight digital compression grain, warm slightly saturated colors, 9:16 vertical format`); (6) organic editing (hard cuts, 1 to 2 intentional jump cuts, varied durations, rhythm by platform: TikTok 15 to 30 s fast, Reels 30 to 60 s medium, Shorts 45 to 60 s informative, Stories 15 s casual); (7) persona and product in real use (everyday clothes, natural hair, `natural skin texture, pores visible, no beauty filter`, tactile interaction with the product). Testimonial only real or clearly staged by an actor; fiction never presented as a real client. **Sequence by format:** unboxing = reaction to the package, opening with effort, 1st contact, sensory reaction, shows to camera; review = look at camera, context, use, short caveat, CTA from the copy; before and after = same light, angle and clothes. Post: caption per §6.1 and low ambient audio.
- **Cinematic prompt** (continuous prose in English, 80 to 150 words, present-tense verbs, no bullets inside the final prompt, 1 main action per scene and a fixed **Character Sheet** repeated across the project's scenes): `1 Shot type & camera → 2 Subject & physical action → 3 Setting & 3-plane environment → 4 Lighting (source, direction, Key:Fill ratio, Kelvin) → 5 Color palette & mood → 6 Lens & depth of field (24mm / 40mm anamorphic / 50mm / 85mm) → 7 Camera movement → 8 Atmosphere & texture (rain, steam, dust, 16mm / 35mm grain) → 9 Visual style reference → 10 Duration & pacing`. **Anti-slop filter:** the empty words `cinematic, epic, stunning, masterpiece, beautiful lighting, dynamic camera` are forbidden: describe the real light, lens and movement. No negations in the prompt (describe what is in the scene); negation only in the engine's negative field. Keep the **consistency bible** in `Trabalho\`.
- **Adaptation per engine:** Veo 3 / 3.1 = long scenes, coherent physics and synchronized audio (for UGC require `"phone camera quality, casual home lighting, not cinematic"`); Kling 2.6 / 3.0 Master = product image-to-video and character consistency (`[Character A: ...]`, `cfg_scale` 0.3 to 0.5 for freedom or 0.7 to 0.9 for adherence + negative prompt); Seedance 2.5 / v1 Pro = natural body movement, UGC and multi-shot up to 30 s with dialogue `{ Personagem: "fala" }`; Sora 2 = continuous camera and smooth spatial transitions (4, 8 or 12 s); MiniMax Hailuo = volumetric physics and camera vectors (`[Pan left]`, `[Dolly in]`, `[Orbit]`, intensity 1 to 10); Runway = controlled camera and editing; Luma = creative camera; Pika = fast iteration with a short prompt. External platforms are options from the list in `operations.md` §0.6, never a fixed route.
- **Production script (references in `references\huashu\`):** per-scene storyboard with camera and acceptance frame (`storyboard-basics.md`), budget and camera grammar (`camera-language.md`), pitfalls (`animation-pitfalls.md`, `animation-best-practices.md`), product interface as protagonist (`ui-demo-animation.md`), GSAP recipes (`gsap-recipes.md`) and export (`video-export.md`). Those files cite the source project's `design_lint`, HyperFrames, ready SFX and BGM and watermark: none of that comes along; audio follows §3 and third-party credit does not enter the piece (`operations.md` §6). **GIF with optimized palette:** `assets\huashu\convert-formats.sh` (2 passes: `palettegen` with `stats_mode=diff` and `paletteuse` with bayer dither; with no ffmpeg on PATH use the one from `imageio-ffmpeg`). Animated cursor and device frames (`cursor.jsx`, `ios_frame.jsx`, `android_frame.jsx`, `macos_window.jsx`, `browser_window.jsx`) are JSX from the framework path; in plain HTML they serve as measure and structure.

## 8. Gates (each with a command; failed = do not show and do not say done)
| Gate | Command or proof | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| **The 6 elements** present and checked: cuts (editorial), transitions, effects, motion graphics anchored to the word, dynamic caption, sounds | item-by-item check; an absent or static element goes back | a Reels once shipped with only caption, frame and pauses |
| **Decoded video** | `assets\check_video.py <mp4> --intro <s> --end <s>` (frame 0, pauses, animated entrance and exit) | a custom pipeline once let through pauses, caption under the gradient and a gray first frame |
| **Safe zone and face** | `assets\reel_assembly\check_safe_zone.py`, the engine's `verify.py`, snapshot at each beat and transition; a frame with anything over the face fails | pieces outside the safe zone and a still circle have already failed |
| **Measured level** | `ffmpeg loudnorm` on the final file (§3) | audio assumed, not measured |
| **Real encoder** | encoder log or `nvidia-smi`, not assumption | the encode once stayed on the CPU with the GPU free |
| **Exported file** | `quality.md` §K (`assets\check_export.py`) | the exporter changes color, range and duration without warning |
| **Self-critique** | list the **3 worst problems** against the 6 elements and the gates and fix them (up to 2 rounds); show what was fixed; no numeric score, which inflates | avoids showing the first render |
| **New motion** | measure the difference against the previous version (`quality.md` §C) before showing | the user's "it looks the same" is a proof failure |
| **High model effort** | only the top model at high effort reaches the level | the result does not come from a loose prompt |
Approval by **cards** before render and V1 preview with a change-request field: `template\tools\cards.mjs`. General preview gate and **frequency gate** (how often the user triggers this decides how much to animate) with the weight per context: `quality.md` §C.
