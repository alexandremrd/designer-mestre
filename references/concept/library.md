# Concept photography library · attachment of `image-product.md` §5
Opened only at step 3 (compose the scene). Optics, materials, and Pass³ live in `image.md` Part B; here only what changes a product's scene.

## 1. The product's natural world (starting point, not destination)
Question: where, when, and with what elements does this product live? The element of use enters as **environment** (it does not count as an anomaly). The category's obvious opens the list; the final idea goes a step beyond it.
| Category | Natural elements of use |
|---|---|
| Skin cleansing, bath, moisturizer | water, drops, light foam, steam, wet stone, leaves, product partially submerged or at the water's edge |
| Perfume, candle, aroma | light passing through glass, thin smoke, flower or resin, fabric, time of day, skin |
| Semi-jewelry, watch | light grazing the metal, skin, fabric, stone, reflection, intimate scale (hand, neck) |
| Food, beverage | ingredient of origin, ice and condensation, table in use, steam, window light |
| Technology, tool | rim light, dark or industrial surface, geometry, the gesture it solves |
| Fashion, footwear, bag | fabric in motion, real street or interior, architecture, wind |
| Home, decor | window light by hour, textures (linen, wood, ceramic), everyday use |
| Supplement, health | natural origin of the ingredients, routine, clean morning light |
| Vehicle, machine | road, hangar, or track with motivated light, human scale, suggested movement |
Submerged, floating, or splashing only when the product withstands that in real life and the scene has a visible cause (water surface, jet origin, coherent gravity). Liquid without an origin, a drop frozen for no reason, and a product "pasted" into the scene are the defects that make a crude image.

## 2. Concept families (vary the family, not just the setting)
Element of use · metaphor of the benefit (what the client feels afterward) · narrative moment (before, during, after) · scale and contrast (giant and tiny, heavy and light) · magnified texture and material · environment that translates the brand · play of light and shadow. Each candidate is a sentence "product + idea through a visual relation".

## 3. Scene and integration (what separates concept from cutout)
Photography (optics, depth of field, motivated light, contact shadow, Fresnel, angle by category) is already in `image.md` Part B §§2–3 and Part C; do not repeat. Only what is missing for the product to live in the scene goes here.
- **Product with a function in the scene:** real contact (rests, sinks, leans, is touched), shadow and reflection from the same light as the scene, believable scale, product legible despite shallow focus. Floating without cause, shadow from elsewhere, or a cut-out edge is rejected.
- **Background and surface:** usage setting, texture that dialogues with the material (marble, wood, stone, fabric, water), gradient from the KV palette or soft bokeh. A flat background only when the idea calls for deliberate emptiness.
- **Composition:** thirds (product at the intersection), leading lines that guide to the product, negative space (product off-center, breathing room for copy and a luxury air), odd numbers in groups; centered only for deliberate calm and symmetry.
- **Consistency on the same product:** same light and palette in all images, different concepts.

## 4. Prompt and generation
Prose, precision, positive exclusion, reference roles, one change per pass, and batch with selection are in `image.md` Part B §1. Specific to this delivery: state the use ("site hero, space on the right for the title"), because it changes the framing; one option per prompt, since alternatives in the same prompt collapse into one; a series of the same product opens with a continuity sentence ("same session as the approved image: same product, same light, same palette"). Local engine (klein 4B): `--ref` with the product first, ~1 min per image; it serves drafts, composition, and cheap redo, while a scene with several products or final quality follow the recipe of the module's §3.

## 5. Beauty and AI look (before showing)
Rejected even with correct physics: manual-style icon or illustration, large or literal prop, perfect symmetry with saturated color, scene without tension or hierarchy, generic "premium" beige, artificial halo or spotlight. AI signs: plastic skin, CG shine, material without wear, cloned repetition, fake bokeh, floating contact, light that comes from nowhere. One localized correction per attempt; if it fails, change the concept.

## 6. User feedback → action
"Next/another" = new concept (change 3 of 4: element, relation, environment, framing/light), not a retouch. "I get it, but it's not interesting" = a more natural idea, do not polish the same one. "It's not beautiful" = aesthetic rejection: redo the scene. "It looks fake" = rebuild the light with a single source. "I liked it" = record why it worked (the mechanism) and carry that forward, not the object. "Based on the first image" = lock the composition and change only what was cited.
