# /product-concept

Shortcut: it has no rules of its own. Load the core `SKILL.md` of the `designer-mestre` skill (sibling folder of this one, in the same skills directory) and the support files it points to (`delivery.md`, `operations.md`, `standards.md`), and follow them in full, because authorization, client collection, spending, one-time approval, and wrap-up live there. Then open `modules/image.md` Parts A, B, and E, `ads.md` §1 + `image.md` Part C (it is a brand image), `modules/image-product.md` §§1–3 (product sources, generator gate, and real label) and §5, which owns this delivery (it covers both creating a concept from scratch and placing 1..N products in a scene sent with `/input`), with the library `references/concept/library.md` only when composing a new scene. Approval (side-by-side board per slot `1..N`) and isolated redo per product follow the `/360-product` pattern.

Free arguments: `/input` (optional, locks the concept image sent), client, folders/names of 1..N products, and concept. Without `/input`, the AI chooses the concept by studying the product, the client, and the history; with `/input` or an attached concept image, it preserves the scene and places all listed products with original, legible packaging and labels. Without a clear client or product, ask once.
