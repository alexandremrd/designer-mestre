# /360-product

Shortcut: it has no rules of its own. Load the core `SKILL.md` of the `designer-mestre` skill (sibling folder of this one, in the same skills directory) and the support files it points to (`delivery.md`, `operations.md`, `standards.md`), and follow them in full, because authorization, client collection, spending, one-time approval, and wrap-up live there. Then open `modules/image.md` Parts A, B, and E (and C when the scene is advertising) and `modules/image-product.md`, which owns this delivery.

Free arguments: client, product, and delivery (`board`, `spin`, `exploded`, `cutaway`). Without a clear client or product, ask once. Without a stated delivery, start with the board, because the spin and the variants depend on it being approved.
