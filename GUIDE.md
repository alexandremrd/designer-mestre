# Agent guide · suggestions without a client
> Read at the start of each request. Written by /aprenda at the end of each project: merge instead of stacking, ceiling ~1.5 KB. They are tendencies of what was approved or rejected across several clients, not laws; safety, logo, copy, and spending limits stay in the core.

## Fixed user rule (all clients)
- If the collection does not meet the need, research and download on your own free resources suited to the narrative and the KV, verifying commercial use and recording origin/license. Free search and download do not require new confirmation; spending, sign-ups, and sending private material require authorization.
- H1 without a final period, in every piece (static, motion, carousel), even when the doc's copy carries the period. Applies to the headline and to on-screen phrases in the H1 role; supporting text and narration keep the punctuation. (23/10/2026)
- Video: caption on 1 line, max. 3 words per block; visual cuts with a short cross dissolve, preserving whole words and pauses; audio transition only in silence. Holds above the briefing. (23/10/2026)
- Pulse = smooth scale with a light glow behind; the element changes neither color nor opacity. (24/10/2026)

## Tends to work
- List the client's or product's folder again before each round: references often arrive or get cropped midway through the work.
- The client's own real photos as the source of paint and shape; datasheet only to understand what the product is.
- Check several variations on a single board and crop the area being checked.
- Deliver every preview in a format the agent opens (file card, explorer with the file selected, or clickable link), plus the path to copy; a rejected one is deleted when replaced, except an AI-generated image.
- On the PC, only what is script (cutout, mask, assembly, conversion, export).
- Decision question with the excerpt of each source and the effect of each option.
- Visual idea before arrangement: "quote card" is a format, not a direction; literal copy does not call for a literal illustration.
- A bold editorial reference indicates ambition, not a preset: adapt the mechanism, never the identity, the layout, or the copy.
- One piece does not prove a brand system; repetition in a campaign is a KV hypothesis, not a rule.

## Tends to be rejected
- Tight composition: stacked blocks with short breathing room and similar weights. Separate groups with space of at least 2× the internal one, contrast weights (Bold × Regular/Medium), and vary scale or alignment to create a path, instead of a uniform stack.
- CTA, badge, or notice as loose text when the KV has a pill component: use the KV's pill, even if the briefing describes only "a line".
- Vague motion feedback ("more dynamic", "it looks bad") answered with many effects at once. Change one layer per round (rhythm, transition, or entrance) and, if the direction is not clear, show 2 short paths before redoing everything.
- In Product/Character 360, the error is uncorrected identity drift: complete the missing angles per U12 and approve the board; originals and safe sources prevail over generated views.
- Weak local image generator for the final image: many rounds and wrong proportion.
- Repeating the same attempt more than 2 times without asking for a reference.
- Copy, element, or detail created without a request.
- Turning on a service, plugin, connector, or memory without warning the cost.
